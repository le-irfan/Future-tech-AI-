AI TIMELINE

The Evolution of Artificial Intelligence

Explore the major milestones that shaped artificial intelligence from its early beginnings to the future.

1950

The Beginning

Alan Turing introduced the idea of machines being able to think and proposed what became known as the Turing Test.

1956

AI Gets Its Name

The term Artificial Intelligence was introduced at the Dartmouth conference, establishing AI as a field of research.

1997

Deep Blue

IBM's Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov, demonstrating the power of specialized computer systems.

2012

Deep Learning Boom

Deep neural networks achieved major breakthroughs in image recognition and accelerated modern machine learning.

2020s

Generative AI

AI systems became capable of generating text, images, audio, video and code, bringing AI into everyday life.

Future

What's Next?

AI continues to evolve alongside robotics, autonomous systems, advanced computing and emerging technologies.
