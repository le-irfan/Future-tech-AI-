AI in Smart Homes

Smart homes combine connected devices, sensors and Artificial Intelligence to automate everyday tasks and make homes more responsive to their occupants.

Smart Lighting

Intelligent lighting systems can automatically adjust lights based on schedules, movement, brightness and user preferences.

Smart Climate Control

AI-powered heating and cooling systems can learn patterns and adjust temperature settings to improve comfort and reduce unnecessary energy use.

Smart Security

Smart cameras, sensors and connected locks can monitor a home and identify unusual activity.

Voice Control

Voice assistants allow people to control compatible devices using spoken commands, such as turning lights on or adjusting the temperature.

Smart Appliances

Connected appliances can automate household tasks, including robotic vacuum cleaners, smart washing machines and intelligent kitchen appliances.

Energy Management

AI can analyze energy consumption and help households identify patterns and optimize when connected devices use electricity.
