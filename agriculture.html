AI in Agriculture

Artificial Intelligence is helping farmers analyze crops, monitor fields, manage resources and make better decisions about agricultural production.

Precision Farming

Precision farming uses data, sensors and AI to understand differences between areas of a field and apply resources more efficiently.

Crop Monitoring

AI can analyze images from cameras, drones and satellites to monitor crop growth and identify changes in fields.

Pest Detection

Computer vision systems can analyze plants and identify visual signs that may indicate pests or plant diseases.

Smart Irrigation

AI can combine information about soil, weather and crops to help determine when and where water may be needed.

Yield Prediction

Machine learning models can analyze historical agricultural data and environmental conditions to estimate potential crop yields.

Autonomous Farming

AI-powered agricultural machines can assist with tasks such as planting, monitoring, harvesting and moving materials around farms.
