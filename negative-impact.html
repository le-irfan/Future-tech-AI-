Negative Impacts of AI

Misinformation

AI can generate incorrect or misleading information, making it important to check important information carefully.

Privacy Concerns

AI systems may process large amounts of information, creating concerns about how personal data is collected and used.

AI-Generated Spam

AI can produce large amounts of low-quality content, which can make the web harder to navigate.

Job Changes

Automation can change the types of tasks people perform and may reduce demand for some repetitive jobs.

Bias

AI can reflect biases found in its training data, which may lead to unfair or inaccurate results.
